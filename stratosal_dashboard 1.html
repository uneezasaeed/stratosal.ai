Core finding
StratoSal AI demonstrates that temperature above 30°C combined with anomalous monsoon rainfall precedes dengue outbreak peaks by 4–6 weeks across Punjab Pakistan — validated across seven years of satellite climate data including the catastrophic 2022 and 2025 flood seasons. This lead time is sufficient for district health officers to pre-position supplies, alert Lady Health Workers, and protect children before the first patients arrive at clinics.
📍
Why Punjab, and why now
Punjab is Pakistan's most populous province — home to over 110 million people and the country's highest recorded dengue burden. It is also the geography where ERA5 satellite climate data, Pakistan NIH disease surveillance, and district-level health infrastructure are most accessible and reliable. Beginning with Punjab allows rigorous validation of the climate-disease correlation model before expanding to Sindh, KPK, and broader South Asia. This is a deliberate beachhead, not a limitation.
