Climate suitability
Scientific foundation
StratoSal AI converts ERA5 satellite reanalysis climate data into district-level dengue transmission risk across Punjab, Pakistan. Temperature in the 26 to 35°C band, relative humidity above 65 percent, and monsoon rainfall that leaves standing water together define the conditions in which Aedes aegypti populations expand. Because those conditions appear in the climate record weeks before cases appear in clinics, they provide a 4 to 6 week window in which district health officers can act. The approach follows the published spatial-temporal evidence linking climate to dengue spread in Pakistan.
👶
Why children, and why the lead time matters
Severe dengue carries its heaviest consequences for children, who are more likely to progress to plasma leakage and shock and less likely to be brought for care early. The warning window StratoSal AI targets is designed to trigger pre-emptive child health actions: oral rehydration salt pre-positioning, Lady Health Worker activation for household fever surveillance, rapid diagnostic test distribution to primary clinics, and larval source reduction around schools. Each of these is deliverable inside a four week window. Warning that arrives after the first cases reach hospital arrives too late to change the outcome.
📍
Why Punjab, and why now
Punjab is Pakistan's most populous province and has repeatedly carried the country's largest share of the national dengue burden, including roughly half of all recorded national cases in the 2021 epidemic. It is also the geography where ERA5 climate data, National Institute of Health surveillance reporting, and district health infrastructure are most accessible together. Beginning in Punjab allows the climate-disease relationship to be validated rigorously before extension to Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and the wider region. This is a deliberate starting point, not a limitation.

Data and reproducibility

Climate inputs are ERA5 monthly means produced by ECMWF and distributed through the Copernicus Climate Change Service Climate Data Store, dataset reanalysis-era5-single-levels-monthly-means, at 0.25° resolution over 29.5°N to 34.0°N and 69.5°E to 75.0°E. Relative humidity is derived from 2 m temperature and 2 m dewpoint using the Magnus formula, Alduchov and Eskridge (1996). Dengue surveillance figures are provincial totals reported by the National Institute of Health Islamabad, WHO, and the peer-reviewed literature, with the reporting date recorded for every year. Complete citations for every figure on this page are in data/SOURCES.md. The extraction notebook that generates data/forecast.json is published in this repository.
